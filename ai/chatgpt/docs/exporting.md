# Exporting a conversation

Open a conversation and click the download icon in the header. Pick a format:

- **Markdown** keeps headings, lists, tables and code blocks with their language.
- **Plain text** strips the formatting.
- **Word** and **PDF** are generated in your browser from the Markdown.
- **JSON** lists each message with its role and Markdown.

The export is built from what is on the page, so scroll to load a very long conversation first.
