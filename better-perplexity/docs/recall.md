# Recall

Recall keeps a searchable history of what you sent in Perplexity. Press **Alt + Shift + H** to open it, type a few words, and press Enter to put the message back in the box. Nothing is sent automatically.

## Find a message

Type part of a message, a thread title or a date. The list shows the newest matches first, with the thread it came from and the date. Click a result to load it, or use the arrow keys and Enter.

## Reuse the last message

With an empty message box, press **ArrowUp** to bring back your last sent message. Press **ArrowUp** again to go further back, and **ArrowDown** to come forward. Press **Escape** to clear the box. This works even after a reload, because the history is stored in your userscript manager.

## What is kept

- **What you sent:** the text of every message, up to 500 per thread and 2,000 overall.
- **Where it went:** the thread it was sent in, so you can find it again.
- **When:** the date and time, so you can search by day or month.

## Privacy

Everything stays in your userscript manager's storage on your device. Nothing is uploaded anywhere, and nothing is shared between browsers unless your manager syncs its own storage.

## Clear it

Use **Clear Recall History** from the userscript menu, or the trash button in the Recall panel. This removes every stored message and cannot be undone.
