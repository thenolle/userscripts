# Code of conduct

This project follows the [Contributor Covenant 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/) as its standard of behavior.

In short: be respectful, assume good faith, welcome people of every background, give and accept feedback graciously, and keep discussion about the work. Harassment, discrimination, personal attacks and publishing others' private information are not acceptable.

## Enforcement

Report problems privately through a [security advisory](https://github.com/thenolle/userscripts/security/advisories/new) or by contacting [@thenolle](https://github.com/thenolle). Maintainers may edit or remove comments, issues and contributions, and may temporarily or permanently ban people who break these rules.
